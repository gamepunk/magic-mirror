Good news! The Magic Mirror browser extension is now live on the Chrome Web Store, completely free.

Click the toolbar icon, pick any region of a webpage, and watch it mirror in a floating window — with multi-page combining, scroll-to-zoom, and drag-to-reorder.

Everything is processed locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

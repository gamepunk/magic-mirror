Good news! The Magic Mirror browser extension is now live on the **Chrome Web Store**, completely free.

## What it can do

- **Live mirror of any webpage region**: Click the toolbar icon, pick any region on a page, and a floating window shows the real pixel-perfect picture instantly — identical to the source
- **Combine elements from multiple pages**: Turn on cross-page mirroring to place elements from different pages into one floating window
- **Scroll to zoom**: Hover over the mirror and scroll to zoom; it stays crisp when enlarged
- **Drag to reorder**: Drag items inside the window to rearrange their order
- **Rich settings**: multi-select mode, mirror count limit, refresh rate, click-to-remove, dark window, and more

## How to install

1. Open the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jfapgohnocglaaminogpmjgabkokgnfl) and search for **Magic Mirror**
2. Click “Add to Chrome”
3. Click the toolbar icon, pick a region on the page, and start mirroring

## Privacy

Everything is processed **locally** in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

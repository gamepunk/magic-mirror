Magic Mirror macOS App **0.0.1** is officially here! Turn anything on your screen into a floating live mirror.

## What it can do

- **Window / region mirroring**: Mirror any app window, or drag to capture any region inside a window (circular selection supported)
- **Multiple container modes**: one floating window per mirror / group by app / unified container
- **Opacity & click-through**: adjust opacity on any mirror, or let clicks pass through
- **Scroll to zoom**: hover and scroll to zoom — stays crisp when enlarged (dynamic capture resolution)
- **Global hotkeys**: show/hide, mirror current window, capture region, pin/close/opacity/click-through
- **Energy saving**: automatically lowers frame rate on low battery or overheating

## How to install

1. Download the DMG (or ZIP) from the [download page](/magic-mirror/en/mac/)
2. Open the disk image and drag **Magic Mirror** into “Applications”
3. On first use, grant access in “System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording” and restart the app

You can also install via Homebrew:

```bash
brew tap gamepunk/tap
brew install gamepunk/tap/magic-mirror
```

A **7-day free trial** is included. After the trial ends, purchase a license key to keep using it ([pricing](/magic-mirror/en/pricing/)). Everything is processed locally — privacy by design.

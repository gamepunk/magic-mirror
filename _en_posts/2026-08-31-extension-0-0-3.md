Magic Mirror browser extension **0.0.3** is out — this release is all about **mirroring any region of a page**, not just elements.

## What's new

- **Mirror a region**: drag-select any rectangular area of a page (charts, canvases, K-line graphs — even places where elements are hard to click). The region is anchored to the page content, so it follows as the page scrolls, and it works across pages too.
- **Two pick modes, one click apart**: "Smart select" (hover & click an element) and "Drag select" (drag to frame a region) — switch between them directly from the toolbar popup.
- **Multi-select now works for both**: keep clicking elements *or* keep drag-selecting regions in one session.
- **Smoother mirrors**: content appears right away when a mirror is created, and stays stable even on busy pages.
- **Cleaner popup**: leaner settings, consistent button styling, and a handy link to the macOS desktop app.

## How to install / update

1. Open the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/magic-mirror/jfapgohnocglaaminogpmjgabkokgnfl) and search for **Magic Mirror**
2. Click "Add to Chrome" — updates install automatically
3. Click the toolbar icon, choose **Smart select** or **Drag select**, and start mirroring

## Privacy

Everything is processed **locally** in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

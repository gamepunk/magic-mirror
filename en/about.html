About

Turn anything on your screen into a floating live mirror.

Magic Mirror is a screen mirroring tool: any region of a webpage, any app window, or any part of your screen — mirrored live into always-on-top floating windows.

Privacy first — everything is processed locally; nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked.
Local-first — no accounts, no backend server, no usage data collection.
Respect users — no ads, no dark patterns.

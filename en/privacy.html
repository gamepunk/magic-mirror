Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Magic Mirror respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Chrome extension and the macOS app access, and how it is handled.

In one sentence: frame content is processed entirely locally — this product never collects, stores, uploads, or sells your screen data. The only exception is anonymous diagnostic information sent when the macOS app crashes (see below), which never contains frame content.

Scope

This policy applies to both products:

  • Chrome Extension (Magic Mirror) — mirrors regions of webpages into a floating window inside Chrome.
  • macOS App (Magic Mirror) — mirrors any window or screen region into a floating on-top window.

Screen content (mirrored frames)

When you create a mirror, the product reads the rendered pixels of the selected area to display them in the floating window.

  • This data is used only to draw the mirror you requested and is processed in memory only.
  • Captured frames are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.
  • The product cannot read page DOM, form data, or clipboard content, and never persists any frames.

Tab titles & URLs (Chrome extension only)

When you combine content from multiple pages into one window, the extension reads the title/URL of joining tabs, solely to label the source on mirror tiles. This is used for on-screen labels only and is never stored or transmitted.

Settings (Chrome extension only)

The extension stores settings locally in the browser to remember your preferences across sessions. This data stays on your device.

Crash diagnostics (macOS app only)

The macOS app integrates Sentry crash reporting: only when the app crashes, an anonymous diagnostic report (crash stack, app version, and OS version) is sent automatically to help fix issues. It never contains frame content, files, or personal information. No network requests are made during normal use.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads

This product contains no analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party code; there is no account system, no backend server, and no usage data collection.

Policy changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated and the new version published at this address.
